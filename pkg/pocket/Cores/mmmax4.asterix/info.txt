Asterix (World EAD), Konami 1992
Analogue Pocket openFPGA port by mmmax4

D-pad: move; B/X: punch; A/Y: jump.
Select: coin; Start: start/select. Dock: 2 players.
Original EEPROM-backed Test menu; save128 bytes.
High-score persistence is not supported.
ROMs and saves are not included.

0.1.4: centered KONAMI sprite clipping corrected;
v013 forward speech decoder retained.
User confirms logo on Pocket; see validation scope.

FX68K: Jorge Cwik. T80: preserved contributor notices.
